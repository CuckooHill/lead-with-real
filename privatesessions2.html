Absolutely. Most people are first-timers. You don't need to do anything but relax.

Do I have to lie down?

No. Lying down is most popular, but you're welcome to sit in a chair or cushion.

Is it safe with health conditions or pregnancy?

Sessions are gentle, but please tell me about any medical conditions, pregnancy, epilepsy or implants before booking so I can adapt.

Yes — 1-2-1 sound journeys and coaching work well online, for clients across the UK and abroad.
