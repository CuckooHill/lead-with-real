The AI Leverage Gap

AI is leverage. It's not a strategy.

Harvard Business Review, 2023

Humans with AI will replace humans without it

HBS's Karim Lakhani put it plainly: AI won't replace people - but people who know how to direct it will replace those who don't. The advantage doesn't go to whoever adopts the tools first. It goes to whoever builds the judgement to run them well.

McKinsey, 2026

Only 6% of companies are actually seeing the payoff

Nine in ten businesses now use AI regularly, and 37% attribute some financial impact to it - but only 6% count as genuine high performers seeing significant returns. Access to the tools stopped being the differentiator years ago. Whether someone is running a real system with them hasn't.

Edelman, 2025

Trust in AI doubles the moment a leader is honest about it

Employee trust in workplace AI jumps from 33% to 68% once they've actually seen it help - yet 70% believe leaders aren't being straight with them about what it means for their jobs. The bottleneck to AI actually paying off isn't the technology. It's whether a leader is visible and honest enough to bring people with them.

The Authenticity Imperative

AI content is everywhere - your real voice is your edge.

The Drum, 2026

Creativity is marketing's last real moat

Performance media is optimised. Targeting is automated. Copy production is commoditised. Efficiency is the new baseline. What still resists automation is a genuine point of view - an idea that makes someone pause, share or act. That only comes from a real voice.

Edelman / DSMN8, 2024

A visible leader is now a buying signal

71% of consumers say they're more likely to buy from a company whose CEO is active on social media. Executive visibility has moved past personal branding - it now shows up directly in purchase decisions.

Forbes, 2026

AI content is actively weakening brand trust

Marketers report AI-generated content weakens brand trust when it lacks a genuine point of view. As production costs fall, differentiation shifts toward expertise, recognisable voice and real proof. The brands winning won't produce the most - they'll produce what feels impossible to automate.

The Business Case

The numbers don't lie

Intuit, 2026

SMEs using AI are growing faster - and hiring more

Drawing on 34,000 business owners and 5.3 million QuickBooks companies. AI adoption correlates with revenue growth, not workforce reduction. At SME level, AI is a growth lever - the businesses benefiting most are scaling output, not just cutting costs.

Gartner, 2026

Differentiation is the real content bottleneck

Companies with a strong, clearly differentiated brand strategy are twice as likely to exceed their growth goals - yet 84% of companies are stuck in what Gartner calls a "brand doom loop," underinvesting in measuring whether their content is actually working. More output isn't the fix. A distinct point of view is.

Content Marketing Institute, 2026

Thought leadership only works if the leader shows up

96% of B2B companies create thought leadership content, but at over a third of them, fewer than 5% of employees actually participate in it. LinkedIn remains the single most effective distribution channel by a wide margin.

The Leadership Gap

Leaders are the bottleneck. Not the tools.

McKinsey, 2026

Leadership is the one thing AI can't automate

As AI takes on more of the work, McKinsey argues the qualities that remain uniquely human become the real competitive edge - reading the room, building trust, and doing the inner work required to lead others well. Leadership, not the algorithm, is what organisations will be judged on.

Read the article →
Gallup, 2026

70% of team engagement comes down to one person

Gallup's research consistently finds the single greatest driver of a team's engagement - and the performance that follows - is the quality of the person leading it. Highly engaged teams show 18% higher sales productivity than the rest. Only 22% of managers worldwide are currently engaged themselves, down from 31% in 2022.

DDI Global Leadership Forecast, 2025

71% of leaders report higher stress since stepping up

Surveying over 10,000 leaders worldwide, the research found the strain comes from decision fatigue and emotional labour - not simply longer hours. Left unaddressed, it's a direct risk to the resilience and profitability leadership is meant to protect.
