Meet King Yin

IMAGE: portrait of King Yin

Jon - known as King Yin in his wellness work - works intuitively, tailoring every session to what a client actually needs in the room, not a fixed script. For 20 years he has travelled to study with the foremost masters in meditation, breathwork, sound healing, yoga, tai chi, reiki, Tibetan Buddhism and Dzogchen.

Discrete and professional, he has trusted experience working with entertainment clients, entrepreneurs, leaders, VIPs and their teams.

King Yin is the Chinese part of Jon's name, which he was guided to use more when doing wellness work. He naturally creates and holds a container for clients to experience what they need, with immediate results and long-lasting effects.

From his broad experience in both the corporate and spiritual worlds, he has created a system and series of programmes that distill the essence of his training into a powerful, effective method - tackling universal and highly personal issues and states.

1:1 Sessions

Sound healing, shamanic clearing techniques and intuitive bodywork - bringing clarity, releasing tension, and helping clients feel grounded, centred and present.

IMAGE: 1:1 session in progress - instruments, hara massage space, or client setting

We start by creating a safe container and opening a dialogue to clarify intentions for the session. Guided meditation, sound and breath help with grounding, centring and energetically clearing blocks.

Optional abdominal massage, in the area known as the hara, works on releasing emotional blocks and tensions. There may be some emotional release - the space is held clearly, gently and safely to ensure this happens smoothly.

We close with sound healing and sacred music.
