About King Yin

For twenty years I've studied and practised meditation, sound and voice with teachers around the world, including in the Tibetan Buddhist and Dzogchen traditions. I've worked one to one with private clients and with teams, and shared sound at museums and festivals.

My sessions are led by intuition, not a script. The aim is always the same: a quiet, held space where you can rest, and leave feeling more like yourself.

Questions

What happens in a session?

We start with a short chat about how you're feeling. Then you lie down comfortably while I play shruti box, gong and voice for most of the 90 minutes, and we finish with time to rest and reflect.

Do I need any experience?

No. There's nothing to prepare or get right. Wear something comfortable and come as you are.

Where do sessions take place?

In my home studio in Northwood, or in your own home if you're local.

How many people can join a group?

Up to six in total, including whoever is organising.

Is this a medical treatment?

No. It's a relaxation and wellbeing experience and doesn't replace medical care. If you're pregnant, have a health condition or aren't sure, mention it when you message me.
