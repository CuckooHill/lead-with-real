About King Yin

King Yin (Jon Kwan) brings over 20 years of practice in sound, voice and meditation, and a lifelong relationship with music. His training spans Vipassana meditation, sacred music, sound and breathwork, hatha yoga and Tibetan Buddhist and Dzogchen traditions, drawn from extensive travels to India, Nepal and Mongolia.

A former professional in London marketing and film, he understands the pressures of modern life from the inside — and brings that same care to every session, private or in a group, led by what the room needs rather than a fixed script.

Twenty years of practice in sound, voice and meditation. A music and sound ceremony at Tate Modern. Sound temples at conscious festivals, including Glastonbury.
